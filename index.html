Happiness is a term universally understood to be associated with positive and self fulfilling feelings. As stated by Ban Ki Moon, the former UN Secretary General, in 2014, "happiness is neither luxury nor indulgence, but a fundamental human goal."

In the various articles we read concerning predictors of happiness and overall self satisfaction, we noticed that oftentimes, there were some commonalities in the articles. Firstly, each of the articles discussed the importance of life expectancy, education, and GDP per capita on happiness. Additionally, they discussed more subjective factors such as social support and freedom to make life choices.

This project examines and analyzes the findings of the 2020 World Happiness Report dataset to see what the relations between its 6 explanatory variables and a country's average happiness are, and what conclusions can be drawn about global happiness.
